Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who we are

IBRATECH.AI is an AI video production and content automation business based in Belgium. Contact: info@ibratech.ai.

What data we collect

  • Your phone number and name, when you message us on WhatsApp or Telegram.
  • The content of messages you send us, and files you share for a project.
  • Your e-mail address, if you write to us by e-mail.

Why we use it

To answer your questions, prepare quotes, deliver and support the work you ordered, and send project updates you asked for. We send marketing messages only to people who agreed to receive them, and you can stop at any time by replying STOP.

Who sees it

We do not sell personal data. Messages are handled with the help of service providers that run our messaging and automation (for example WhatsApp/Meta, a WhatsApp Business Solution Provider, and hosting providers), only as needed to provide the service.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed for the purpose above or as required by law. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its use, or withdraw consent. Write to info@ibratech.ai. You may also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be).
